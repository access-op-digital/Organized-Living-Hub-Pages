Laundry Closet

Shelving, Cabinets and Hanging Storage Around Washers and Dryers

Organized Living laundry closet systems arrange shelves, hanging rods, baskets and cabinets around a washer and dryer. Our adjustable freedomRail storage accommodates changing supplies and routines; Select and Classica create built-in storage; fixed shelving keeps an established layout in place. Storage planning starts with the appliances and the space required for their doors, hoses and ventilation. Separate areas for sorting, hanging and supply storage organize each stage of the laundry routine.

Types of Laundry Closet Systems

Choose the mounting system before selecting shelves and accessories. Rail shelving allows repeated rearrangement, built-in cabinetry organizes the opening into dedicated compartments, and fixed shelving suits a layout that will stay in place. A laundry closet can use any of these approaches when the components fit around the appliance installation.

Laundry Closet Shelving and Components

Shelves Above and Beside the Washer and Dryer

A shelf beside the machines keeps frequently used supplies within reach. Shelves above them can hold spare linens or less frequently used items, where the shelves remain within comfortable reach and the appliance clearances remain open. The freedomRail 48-inch Ventilated Shelf comes in 12- and 16-inch depths. Its open wire surface allows air through the shelf; the room still needs the ventilation required by the appliances.

Hanging Rods and Valet Rods

A clothes rod creates a place to hang shirts as they leave the dryer. The freedomRail 30-inch Clothes Rod fits below compatible shelves using the specified Rod Clips and supports. A separate Valet Rod hooks into a freedomRail Upright and slides out for temporary garment handling. Position either rod so hanging clothes clear controls, openings and the floor.

Pull-Out Baskets and OBox Storage

Pull-out baskets bring their contents to the front of the closet, which helps when the appliance occupies the space below a shelf. The freedomRail Chrome Basket with Glide fits the OBox 1 Shelf Unit and uses full-extension glides. Available basket heights are 6 and 13 inches. Match the basket to that OBox and leave enough front clearance to extend it fully.

Doors, Drawers and Sorting Space

Cabinet doors conceal reserve supplies, while drawers separate small laundry items from the larger containers on shelves. In a freedomRail arrangement, OBox Accessory Doors attach to the OBox 1 Shelf Unit. Keep a separate space for collecting laundry: a hamper or removable basket should come out without blocking the machine doors. Choose its size after checking the remaining floor and opening clearance.

Laundry Closet Finishes

White can keep a small appliance opening visually simple; a wood-tone laminate can continue the finish of a nearby hallway or kitchen. freedomRail and Select include options such as White, Chocolate Pear, Century Gray, Slate, Whiskey Walnut and Blonde Oak. Classica offers its own finish and door-style combinations, while Lifetime wire is available in White and Nickel. Check the selected line and component together because finish availability differs.

Laundry Closet Layouts by Size and Appliance Type

Side-by-Side Laundry Closet

A side-by-side pair leaves a broad wall above the machines for shelving or cabinets. Divide that run by use: daily supplies near the loading position, clean folded items in their own section and hanging space where garments will not cover the controls. Check both machine doors with the proposed baskets and drawers extended.

Stacked Washer and Dryer Closet

A stacked pair uses more of the closet height, so the useful storage may sit beside the appliances. A narrow shelf column can keep supplies accessible without requiring a reach over the dryer. Confirm the appliance manufacturer’s permitted stacking arrangement and kit before locating any shelf around it. The closet system supports stored items, not the washer or dryer.

Laundry Closet With a Top-Load Washer

A top-load lid changes where the lowest shelf can sit. Open it fully and measure its complete movement before choosing shelf height or depth. Leave access to the dispenser and controls as well. A side shelf can keep detergent closer when an overhead shelf would interfere with the lid.

Small Hallway Laundry Closet

A hallway installation needs to work when the closet is open and allow the doors to close when the work is finished. Position pull-outs within the accessible opening, accounting for the portion a bifold or sliding door covers. Keep baskets out of the walking route, and check the appliance instructions before adding storage to a door with ventilation openings.

Combined Laundry and Household Storage

A wider utility closet can separate laundry supplies from clean linens and household backstock. Give each category a defined shelf or cabinet so a replacement pack does not displace the everyday products. Follow the detergent label’s storage instructions, and preserve access to water shutoffs, plugs and service connections.

Who We Build Laundry Closet Storage For

Homeowners

Homeowners can arrange the closet around the way laundry moves through the house: collect, wash, dry, fold and put away. Where the layout has room, a properly supported freedomRail Desk Top can provide a folding surface; keep appliance openings and required access clear. Adjustable storage is useful when a new appliance or a different mix of supplies changes the space available.

Builders and Multifamily Teams

For repeated laundry layouts, specify the storage alongside the appliance schedule. Organized Living supports Builders and Multifamily project teams with product selection and Dealer coordination. Why Choose Organized Living for a Laundry Closet

Storage That Follows the Laundry Sequence

Shelves hold supplies, baskets separate loose items, and rods provide temporary hanging space as each load is finished. Combining those functions around the machines reduces the need to borrow a chair or another room for temporary storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What belongs in a laundry closet?

A laundry closet holds the washing and drying appliances plus the storage needed to use them: laundry supplies, a sorting space, shelves for clean items and a rod for garments when the layout permits. Keep the machine controls, connections and required ventilation accessible.

How much space does a laundry closet need?

The appliance dimensions and installation instructions determine the space required. Measure width, height and depth, then account for doors, lids, hoses, ventilation and service access. Choose shelving from the wall space remaining after those requirements are met.

Can I put shelves above a washer and dryer?

Yes, where the appliance installation requirements and available reach allow it. Open a top-load washer’s lid fully before setting the lowest shelf. With a tall stacked pair, side storage may be easier to reach than a shelf above the dryer.

Can I add laundry storage without replacing the whole closet?

Yes. An existing freedomRail installation can accept compatible shelves and accessories, with components that reposition on the installed Rail and Uprights. Identify the existing system first and confirm the part’s stated compatibility before ordering.

How much does a laundry closet system cost?

The price depends on the product line, the length of the storage run, the number of cabinets or pull-outs, and the installation work. Current component prices appear in the shop. For a fitted design, ask an Installing Dealer for a quote that identifies the storage and installation included.

Does Organized Living install the washer and dryer?

No. Organized Living manufactures the storage systems, and its Installing Dealers provide closet installation. Arrange appliance installation and any plumbing or electrical work with the appropriate provider and confirm the responsibilities before work starts.

How long does an order take to arrive?

Available products generally leave the warehouse within 3–5 business days. This is a dispatch timeframe; arrival depends on the product, order size, destination and delivery method. Our shop confirms order timing and shipping eligibility, and an Installing Dealer confirms the schedule for a fitted project.

Which warranty applies to laundry closet shelving?

The warranty follows the product line and its intended use. Open the relevant document on the support page to review the term, installation requirements, exclusions and claim process. Keep the proof of purchase and product details with the warranty records.
