About Organized Living

Organizing homes and improving life since 1919

Organized Living is a manufacturer of closet systems and home storage products, founded in 1919 in Norwood, Ohio and headquartered in Cincinnati. We build seven product lines, from Lifetime™ ventilated wire to the adjustable freedomRail® system and Select® built-ins, at a 680,000 square foot plant in Bloomington, Indiana, and sell them through installing Dealers nationwide and our own online shop. Orders ship across the United States and Canada in 3 to 5 business days, and our software, Design Tool™, Closet Selector® and Bid360®, carries a closet from design through installation.

We manufacture; we do not install. A homeowner designs a closet online and buys direct or through a local Dealer, a Builder or Multifamily developer specifies our systems by the unit, and the Dealer network measures, orders and installs. That division of labor is how a company that started with brass faucets became the largest privately held company in the storage and organization industry.

Our Story: From Schulte Brass to Organized Living

Organized Living began in 1919 as The Schulte Brass Company, when George Schulte and his brother started producing solid brass faucets and plumbing fixtures in their father's garage in Norwood, Ohio. Every chapter since has been the same move repeated: notice a market forming, build the product for it, then build the factory to make it well.

  1. 1919

    George Schulte and his brother start The Schulte Brass Company in a Norwood, Ohio garage, making solid brass faucets and plumbing fixtures.

  2. 1930s

    After Prohibition ends, George patents an ice crusher, then develops the chrome and brass bathroom accessories that replaced tile-matched fixtures. Schulte becomes a plating and casting specialist for manufacturers across Greater Cincinnati.

  3. 1940s

    Corrosion-resistant plating turns most of the factory over to machining parts for military aircraft.

  4. 1950s

    A new factory opens in Oakley, Ohio, for bathroom accessories and contract plating.

  5. 1960s

    Elmer Kokenge marries into the family business, becomes its second president, and installs the company's earliest fully automatic plating lines.

  6. 1970s

    Schulte leads the industry into epoxy powder coating. Elmer strips the vinyl-coated shelving in his condo, re-coats it in epoxy powder, and his neighbors ask for the same. In 1976 the company launches ventilated shelving, applying epoxy powder coating to shelving before any other maker.

  7. 1980s

    A nationwide network of installing Dealers forms, and in 1986 manufacturing expands into Bloomington, Indiana.

  8. 1995

    John Kokenge becomes the third president of a third-generation family business and writes down the vision, mission and values that still run the company. The same year brings the earliest built-in wood shelving line and the acquisitions that widened the range.

  9. 1996

    freedomRail® is invented, combining wood and metal into a fully adjustable storage system. It remains the flagship line.

  10. 2004

    Having outgrown its facility for the fifth time, the company consolidates all U.S. manufacturing into the largest building in Bloomington, over 680,000 square feet.

  11. 2013

    After almost a century, Schulte becomes Organized Living: one brand for the history and the future.

  12. 2016

    Proprietary software renders 3D closet upgrade options for Dealers and Builders, the start of Closet Selector.

  13. 2019

    Select® launches, and the company marks 100 years with its headquarters still in Cincinnati, less than 10 miles from the Norwood garage.

  14. 2021

    VUE® completes the range as a fixed wood shelving system with no exposed hardware.

  15. 2023

    In partnership with Bid360®, the company launches a bid and project management tool for closets in Multifamily and Single Family construction.

The full timeline, with the brochures and factory photographs, is on our History page. →

Vision, Mission and Values

Vision

To be the brand of choice for home storage and organization in the Single Family and Multifamily Markets.

Mission

To lead the market with exceptional Products, Programs, and People that support and grow our Dealers and Market Members.

Five values run the company, and each one shows up somewhere specific on the factory floor.

Do the right thing

We use the thickest steel gauges in our industry rather than the thinnest we could get away with, because thinner steel shortens a product's life.

Act with passion

Packing material from vendor shipments is reused, and our packaging can be on its fourth lifecycle by the time it reaches a job site.

Build winning partnerships

Working with our steel suppliers, we traced a chemical build-up in our cleaning tanks to a protective coating on raw steel and changed the inventory flow so the coating is no longer needed.

Think long term

A forklift bought in 1968 still runs in the Bloomington plant, maintained and re-batteried rather than replaced.

Learn, grow, change, excel

In 2022 the Distribution Center cut backorders to 0.29 percent of sales while shipping 99 percent of orders on time.

How We Build and Test Our Products

We hold no third-party product certifications. What we have instead is documented: engineering testing at the Bloomington facility, published performance specifications, and material standards we can substantiate.

Materials

Steel is 95 to 97 percent recycled content. Thermally fused laminate is 90 to 100 percent post-industrial recovered content plus 5 percent post-consumer.

Coating

A proprietary epoxy powder hybrid coating on steel shelving and hardware, applied and cured with zero waste and zero release of VOCs. Vinyl coating costs less and off-gasses in the home; we do not use it.

Hardware

Every hardware system installs without extra blocking, and our drywall anchors are true hollow wall anchors that rosette behind the wall.

Testing

Load capacity, durability and performance are tested in-house at the plant that builds the product. Published figures: Lifetime™ 75 pounds per linear foot, Shelftech™ 45.

Packaging

We make our own corrugated boxes in-house, cut to the order, and every job box carries a QR code to the installation instructions for the parts inside.

Warranty

Terms differ by product line and are published on our Support page. Damaged parts are exchanged, and in-warranty failures have all parts replaced.

Frequently Asked Questions About Organized Living

Is Organized Living the same company as Schulte?

Yes. The Schulte Brass Company, later Schulte Corporation, became Organized Living in 2013. Replacement parts for ventilated shelving are available through our shop.

Who owns Organized Living?

Organized Living is privately held and family owned, in its third generation. John Kokenge is President and CEO.

Where are Organized Living products made?

At our 680,000 square foot manufacturing and distribution facility in Bloomington, Indiana. Headquarters are in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Does Organized Living install closets?

No. We manufacture the systems. Installation is handled by our nationwide network of installing Dealers, or by the homeowner using the installation resources that ship with each order.

Does Organized Living sell direct?

Yes. Homeowners can buy kits and components at shop.organizedliving.com, or buy an installed closet through a Dealer.

Do you ship to Canada?

Yes. We ship throughout the United States and Canada, generally in 3 to 5 business days for products in stock at the warehouse.

Is the Dealer program a franchise?

No. It is a partnership with no franchise fees and no minimum purchase volume.

Are Organized Living products certified?

We hold no third-party product certifications. Products are engineering-tested in-house for load capacity, durability and performance, and specifications such as the 75 pound per linear foot rating on Lifetime™ are published.
