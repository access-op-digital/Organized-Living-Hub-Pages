Closet systems by housing type

What changes between an apartment, a senior living suite, a student room and a hotel closet is how often the system is reconfigured and how hard it is used.

Apartments
Repeatable storage specifications for apartment layouts, with products selected around resident needs and maintenance requirements.
Senior living
Layouts planned around the applicable accessibility requirements, with reach, approach, clear floor space and mounting heights reviewed by the project designer.
Student housing
Storage planned for compact rooms and frequent turnover. Component selection determines the surfaces, mechanisms and maintenance requirements.
Hospitality
Closet layouts and finishes coordinated with guest-room storage needs and the property’s service and maintenance requirements.
