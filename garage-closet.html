Garage Closet Shelving and Components

Rails, Uprights and Brackets

The Rail anchors the storage system to the wall. Uprights hang vertically from it and receive shelf brackets, while EDGE cabinet units use their own mounting components. Follow the installation instructions for the wall construction and selected parts; the correct Rail, hardware and support arrangement are part of the storage design.

Ventilated and Solid Shelves

EDGE ventilated shelves provide an open steel surface for bins and larger gear. The statement of line lists 12- and 16-inch depths, with matching brackets. Movable laminate shelves are available for the cabinet units and towers. Match the shelf to its intended support rather than choosing by width alone.

Shelf Towers and Cabinet Doors

A shelf tower groups several storage levels within one vertical unit. EDGE offers towers with fixed and adjustable shelves, plus doors sized for the matching tower or cabinet unit. Tower feet provide support at floor level. Check which doors, feet and hardware are included when comparing a complete kit with individual components.

Drawers for Tools and Small Supplies

Drawers keep small hand tools and loose supplies from spreading across a shelf. EDGE drawers fit the lower units and towers and include full-extension, soft-close glides. Full extension brings the drawer contents forward for access; the soft-close mechanism controls the final part of closing.

Bike Hooks, Tool Hooks and Sorting Bins

Dedicated accessories give awkward shapes a defined storage position. The EDGE Bike Hook mounts to the EDGE Rail and holds one bike vertically. A Grip Everything Hook uses a friction grip for handled tools, while Quick Sort Bins organize small parts on their own mounting rail. Select accessories by their stated application, mounting method and individual capacity.

Slate Garage Cabinet Finishes

freedomRail EDGE cabinetry is available in Slate, paired with Granite Gray metal components. Handles and adjustable tower feet complete the cabinet arrangement. The laminate surface gives the storage wall a consistent appearance across drawers, doors and open units; available colors vary by component, so check the finish on each product when ordering additions.

Garage Closet Layouts by Space and Use

A Storage Closet Inside the Garage

An existing garage closet needs an opening wide enough to reach each shelf and remove its contents. Measure the clear doorway as well as the interior walls. Group bins by task on accessible shelves, and check that a drawer or cabinet door can open through the closet entrance before adding one.

Side-Wall Shelving Beside a Parked Car

The useful depth along a side wall depends on the vehicle parked beside it. Check clearance with the car in its normal position and its doors open. A shallower shelf run can provide usable storage where a deeper cabinet would restrict movement, but the stored items and any projecting hooks must fit within that same clearance.

A Cabinet Wall for Household Backstock

Upper units and lower cabinets divide a garage wall into separate storage levels. Use the lower section for frequently retrieved supplies, with less frequent items above. Compare the actual container size with the cabinet interior and door opening to confirm bin access.

A Sports and Bike Storage Zone

Group bikes, helmets and sports bags near the route used to leave the garage. A vertical bike hook needs room for the whole bicycle and the movement required to lift it on and off. Keep nearby shelves and cabinets clear of that path, and check the chosen accessory against the bike or equipment it will hold.

A Tool and Gardening Storage Zone

Long-handled tools use a wall differently from small tools and fasteners. Give handled tools a compatible hook, place hand tools in drawers, and sort loose pieces into bins. This keeps a single task's equipment close together without burying smaller items behind a larger case or container.

A Garage Entry Closet

Near the door into the house, a small shelving and hook arrangement can collect coats, bags, boots and outdoor gear. Plan reachable positions for the people who use it and preserve the door swing and walking route. Leave enough space for items to come off their hooks without brushing the parked vehicle.

Garage Closet Size and Planning Guide

Garage closet dimensions should follow the available wall, the vehicle clearance and the equipment being stored. A closet inside the garage and an open storage wall need different access checks, even when they use the same shelving components.

  1. Measure the usable wall. Record width, height and depth, including baseboards, door trim and wall obstructions.
  2. Mark access requirements. Account for vehicle doors, the garage door and its operating hardware, household doors, outlets and equipment that needs access.
  3. Measure the contents. Include bin handles and lids, tool cases and the full projection of hanging gear.
  4. Check the selected configuration. Review the assembly dimensions, opening space and installation requirements before ordering.

Choose shelf spacing after identifying what each shelf will hold. Leave lifting room above a tote and access room in front of it; a container that fits tightly between shelves may still be difficult to remove.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose open shelves or cabinets for a garage closet?

Choose open shelves when visible, direct access to bins matters most. Cabinets conceal mixed supplies behind doors, and drawers help separate smaller items. A mixed layout can assign each storage type to the items it serves.

Does the kit width equal the width of each shelf?

No. The 42-inch EDGE Essential Kit includes a 42-inch Rail and 30-inch shelves. Compare the kit's overall dimensions with the individual component dimensions when planning a storage run.

Can I add doors or drawers to an EDGE system later?

Yes, compatible doors and drawers are available for designated EDGE units and towers. Match the accessory to the unit's dimensions and intended location before ordering; doors and drawers are not interchangeable across every configuration.

Can I install garage shelving myself?

Yes. Our guides and videos cover installation requirements for compatible systems and wall conditions. Review the chosen configuration’s instructions first. An Installing Dealer can assess the wall conditions and complete the installation.

Is a Rail included with every accessory?

No. For example, the EDGE Bike Hook is sold separately from its Rail. Complete kits list their included Rails and hardware; check the product description when buying individual accessories.

Will any closet accessory fit my garage storage system?

Compatibility depends on the product. EDGE hooks, cabinet doors and drawers specify their mounting points or matching units, while other freedomRail accessories use different supports. Check the listing and installation instructions or ask Customer Support to confirm the part.

Where do I get replacement parts or help with an order?

Replacement parts and accessories are available through the shop. Contact Customer Support with the order information and a description of the part or issue.
