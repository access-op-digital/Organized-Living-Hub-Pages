Choosing a closet system

7 questions

Which Organized Living closet system is right for my space?

System selection starts with the room, the stored items, the required finish and whether the layout needs to change after installation. Organized Living offers adjustable rail storage, built-in cabinetry and fixed shelving.

  • freedomRail for adjustable closet and household storage
  • freedomRail EDGE for modular garage and household gear storage
  • VUE for fixed solid shelving in thermally fused laminate
  • Select and Classica for built-in cabinetry; Select includes limited shelf adjustability
  • Lifetime and Shelftech for fixed ventilated wire storage in suitable spaces

What is the difference between adjustable and fixed closet shelving?

Adjustable storage allows shelves, rods and compatible accessories to be repositioned after the mounting system is installed. Fixed storage follows the specified mounting positions and supports. The distinction concerns the mounting system; the finish and shelf surface are separate choices.

Our freedomRail and freedomRail EDGE collections provide adjustable rail storage. VUE and fixed ventilated wire use fixed supports. Select and Classica provide built-in cabinetry, with some shelf adjustment available in Select configurations.

What is the difference between freedomRail and freedomRail EDGE?

freedomRail is our adjustable system for closets and household storage. freedomRail EDGE is the modular collection for garage equipment and household gear, including documented mudroom applications.

Both use rail-based storage arrangements, but each accessory must match the product line and support named in its instructions. An Installing Dealer can compare suitable configurations for the room.

Is wire shelving or wood shelving better for a closet?

Ventilated wire provides an open shelf surface. Solid laminate provides a continuous surface for folded items and smaller objects, with finishes that coordinate with the room. The stored items, shelf supports, finish and available space determine the appropriate choice.

Our Lifetime and Shelftech collections are fixed ventilated wire systems. VUE is fixed solid thermally fused laminate shelving. Product documents establish load and installation requirements; wire construction alone does not guarantee moisture resistance or a higher capacity.

How much weight can Organized Living shelving hold?

Published capacity depends on the system, shelf span and installation. A wall-system rating is not a rating for every shelf, drawer or accessory. Match each figure to the support arrangement in the applicable specification sheet.

  • Shelftech: up to 45 pounds per linear foot when installed as specified
  • Lifetime: up to 75 pounds per linear foot when installed as specified
  • VUE: consult the current specification sheet for the selected shelf and supports
  • freedomRail: up to 170 pounds per linear foot of wall; individual shelf capacities vary with Upright spacing and shelf configuration

Does Organized Living make custom closets?

Organized Living manufactures configurable storage systems using standard components. Each layout is planned around the room dimensions, available openings and storage requirements. An Installing Dealer can develop the design and identify where a project needs separately specified custom work.

What does Organized Living not supply?

Our product ranges cover shelving, hanging rods, cabinetry, drawers, baskets and compatible storage accessories. Available doors, hardware and component sizes vary by collection; the current statement of line identifies the supplied options.

Lighting, custom millwork and other items outside the selected collection should be specified separately with the project team or Installing Dealer.

Designing and planning a space

6 questions

How do I design my closet with Organized Living?

Our Design Tool supports freedomRail and Select layouts using room measurements and product selections. Designs can be customized, saved and shared for online ordering or Installing Dealer review.

  1. Measure the width, height and depth of the usable walls, including door openings and obstructions
  2. Choose freedomRail or Select and the available design options
  3. Review the arrangement of shelves, rods and accessories against the measurements
  4. Save and share the design, then use the relevant ordering or Dealer route

Is the Organized Living Design Tool free?

Yes. The Design Tool is available to plan a layout, save the design and share it before ordering. Product purchases and any Dealer services follow their separate order or quote terms.

Which product lines can I design online?

The Design Tool covers the freedomRail and Select product lines. For other lines, contact an installing dealer, who will prepare the design after measuring the space.

Does Organized Living offer a free design consultation?

The Design Tool provides an online starting point for freedomRail and Select. Our shop support team can assist with an online design, and Installing Dealers provide local planning support. Confirm the scope and any charges for a Dealer consultation directly with that Dealer.

Can I save and share my closet design?

Yes. Designs created in the Design Tool can be saved and shared for online ordering or Installing Dealer review. A Dealer may need additional room information or field measurements before confirming a fitted quotation.

How should I plan a pantry?

Start with an inventory of containers, appliances and bulk supplies, then arrange the shelves around item height, frequency of use and practical reach. Check shelf depth, door movement, corners and the space needed to extend baskets or drawers.

Reach-in and walk-in pantries can use adjustable or fixed storage. Select the system from the opening, intended load and component specifications, with frequently used items in accessible positions.

How do homebuilders work with Organized Living?

Builders work with Organized Living through a program built for a community or group of homes, developed either directly with Organized Living or with an installing dealer. Closet Selector is Organized Living's digital tool for contracting standard and upgrade closets throughout a home, letting builders present closet options to buyers and capture the upgrade decision. It covers the whole home rather than a single closet, so a buyer can select upgrades room by room.

Can homebuyers upgrade their closets during construction?

The builder sets which standard and upgrade packages are available, and the buyer's selection flows into the order without a separate specification round. Can closets be specified to meet ADA reach ranges?

Closet storage can be designed around the applicable accessibility requirements. The ADA Standards identify a 15-inch minimum and 48-inch maximum for unobstructed adult reach. Forward and side reaches over obstructions have different limits, so the approach and obstruction must be evaluated separately.

The project designer must assess reach, clear floor space, approach and mounting heights together. An adjustable product does not by itself establish a compliant installation.
